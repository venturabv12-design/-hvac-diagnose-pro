Common questions

Mike won't open, or it opens to an old version Delete the app or the home-screen icon, then install again from the App Store or from trazermike.io. That clears a stale cached copy, which is the usual cause.
I can't sign in Use the same email your company set you up with. Forgot your password? Tap Sign in → Forgot password and we'll email you a reset link. If it doesn't arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder, then email us.
How do I get an account? Most technicians are set up by their company. If you want Mike for yourself or for your shop, start at trazermike.io.
Managing or cancelling your subscription Subscriptions are handled on the web, not in the app. Sign in at trazermike.io to change or cancel your plan at any time.
Deleting your account In the app, open the menu and choose Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and your personal data, and cancels any active subscription. It cannot be undone. If you can't sign in, email us from the address on the account and we'll do it for you.

Safety

Mike is a diagnostic and training assistant for working technicians. He is not a substitute for your own judgment, your training, or the manufacturer's instructions. If you smell gas, suspect carbon monoxide, or believe anyone is in danger, leave the area and call 911 or your gas utility's emergency line first.
